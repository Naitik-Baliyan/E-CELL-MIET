The E-Cell (Entrepreneurship Cell) of MIET is a remarkable group of students who are passionate about fostering an entrepreneurial spirit amongst their peers. In a world where startups are increasingly important for driving innovation and solving problems,

E-Cell is working towards providing exposure, insights and awareness related to entrepreneurship. With our events, workshops, informative content, and exciting initiatives, E-Cell is creating a vibrant and dynamic startup ecosystem within MIET. The team's dedication is evident in their remarkable impact over the years.
