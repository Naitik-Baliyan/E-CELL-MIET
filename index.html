What's E-Cell ?

E-Cell MIET helps the hustling startups and young professionals via dynamic workshops, thought-provoking speaker sessions, high-stakes business plan competitions, and numerous other game-changing initiatives throughout the year to create a crucible for innovation. We stand as pillars of support for budding entrepreneurs, providing them with personalized guidance from experienced mentors, crucial funding opportunities, and a robust network that can change the course of their journey forever!

MIET Incubation Forum (MIF)

The MIET Incubation Forum provides a comprehensive ecosystem for student entrepreneurs. It offers state-of-the-art workspace, mentorship from seasoned professionals, and access to seed funding networks.

By connecting students with industry experts and alumni founders, MIF acts as a catalyst for transforming raw ideas into market-ready businesses.

IIC

Institution's Innovation Council (IIC)

IIC is an initiative of the Ministry of Education (MoE) designed to systematically foster the culture of innovation in all Higher Education Institutions (HEIs).

The council organizes periodic workshops, seminars, and interactions with entrepreneurs to create a vibrant innovation ecosystem on campus.
